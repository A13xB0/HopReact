How to read this

A repeater is judged on the most recent thing seen of it — either an advert (it announcing itself, which it does on its own schedule whether or not anyone is using the mesh) or traffic it passed on for other people: {{range $i, $n := stdTypes}}{{if $i}}, {{end}}{{$n}}{{end}}. Either one arriving puts it back to green. Observers are judged on whether they check in, not on what they have heard.

a bit quiet after {{$s.QuietHrs}}h · concerning after {{$s.ConcernHrs}}h · not active after {{$s.AlarmHrs}}h. Hover anything to see what its colour is based on.
